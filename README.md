One Word for the Boss: the program writes exactly one word describing the boss to `/output/entry.txt` — "Micromancer" (11 characters). A micromanager crossed with a necromancer: the boss who conjures new tasks out of thin air, hovers over every detail, and raises dead deadlines back to life just to watch you meet them twice.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
